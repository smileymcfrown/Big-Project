Asymmetric VR / PC · Unity 6.3 LTS · October 2026

Dumpling Kitchen, rebuilt properly

One player is a chef in VR trying to cook dinner in five minutes. Everyone else is a dumpling on a PC, turning off the gas, unplugging the rice cooker, killing the lights and knocking the chef's hat over their eyes. This guide rebuilds it from an empty project, the way a studio would.

  • Unity 6.3 LTS
  • URP
  • OpenXR 1.18
  • XR Interaction Toolkit 3.6
  • Input System 1.20
  • Netcode for GameObjects 2.13
  • Multiplayer Services 2.4 (Relay)

How to use this guide

It's written for you coming back after four years: you know what a GameObject and a prefab are, you've written C#, and you've used Mirror. It explains why as well as what, because the why is what makes the habits stick.

  • Part 1 builds the whole game as a couch game on one PC: the Quest 2 for the chef and split-screen with gamepads/keyboard for the dumplings. No networking yet, so you can focus on clean game code.
  • Part 2 converts it to networked play with Netcode for GameObjects: the chef hosts, dumplings join over LAN or the internet.
  • Every script is complete and copy-pasteable. The same files are in the repo under docs/tutorial/code/ so you can copy whole folders.
  • Tick chapters off in the sidebar as you go. Ticks are saved in this browser only.
  • Prefer a game? The Dumpling Kitchen Quest board (in your claude.ai artifacts, and at docs/tutorial/dumpling-kitchen-quest.html) splits this guide into nine 5-hour services with XP, ranks, boss fights and focus sprints, and has this whole guide built in.

The game you're building

VR · 1 player

The chef

  • Cook the dish on the order ticket: put ingredients in a pot, put the pot on a heat source, wait until cooked (not burnt).
  • Serve: put the cooked ingredients on the serving counter.
  • Fix sabotage by grabbing or pressing the broken thing.
  • Wins by serving 3 dishes before the timer runs out.
PC · 1–4 players

The dumplings

  • Run, jump and climb around the kitchen.
  • Walk up to things and press Interact: gas valve, light switch, plugs, pots, the chef's hat.
  • Win if the chef hasn't served 3 dishes when time runs out.
Round · 5 minutes

The flow

  • Dumplings join. The chef rings the service bell.
  • 3, 2, 1 countdown, then a 5:00 round.
  • Results for 10 seconds, then back to waiting.

The details (how many dishes, how long cooking takes, what each sabotage does) are deliberately simple and live in data assets, so you can redesign them without touching the core code.

What you need

  • A Windows 10/11 PC with a VR-capable GPU. PC VR through Link is Windows only.
  • Your Quest 2, plus either a USB 3 cable (Link) or 5 GHz Wi-Fi (Air Link). Steam with SteamVR installed if you want the SteamVR route.
  • One or more gamepads (any XInput or PlayStation pad), or just the keyboard and mouse.
  • About 25 GB of free disk space, Git with Git LFS, and a Unity ID.
